YOUR WEEK, IN ONE PLACE

Run. Swim. Cycle. One plan.

Athevia gives every day a purpose while keeping the whole week visible. The plan includes sport, duration or distance, workout structure and the details you need before you start.

STRUCTURED, NOT VAGUE

A real session, not just “swim today.”

Planned workouts break the work into clear sets with volume, rest and practical instructions — like breathing-focus lengths, relaxed freestyle and warm-up work.

Week at a glanceSee run, swim, cycle and recovery days together.
Session detailWarm-up, main set, repetitions, distance and rest are visible before training.
Works with your dayStart the workout on iPhone, use Apple Watch where supported, or record completion manually.

MEASURE WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED

Every completed workout becomes evidence.

After a session, Athevia compares the result with the target and surfaces the useful training signals rather than treating completion as a simple checkbox.

Heart rateCadenceEnergyPaceZones

Available metrics depend on the workout source and sensors you use.

THE NUMBERS AREN'T THE WHOLE STORY

Tell Athevia how the workout felt.

Training decisions need context. Athevia lets you report effort, pain, recovery and unusual symptoms after the workout so the training record includes how your body responded.

RPE 1–10How hard did it feel?
Pain 0–10Was there any pain?
RecoveryFresh, good, okay, tired or exhausted.
SymptomsOptional flags for unusual responses.

LEARN FROM THE WEEK

Progress that can change the plan.

Athevia brings completed training, adherence, trends and sport-specific weekly analysis together so you can understand the week — and so the next one has better context.

LOCAL FIRST

Your training starts on your iPhone.

You can continue with the training already on the device. Sign in with Apple when you want account-based cloud backup and restore.

Apple Health and location access are permission-based and are used only for the features you choose to enable.
