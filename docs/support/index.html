Frequently asked questions

Does Athevia require an Apple Watch?

No. Athevia is designed to support iPhone-recorded workouts and imported Health data where available. Some metrics depend on the sensors and workout source you use.

Why is a workout missing?

Check Apple Health permissions, confirm the workout exists in Apple Health, and reopen Athevia so it can import available workout history.

Why is my GPS route missing?

Route recording is supported for applicable outdoor workouts started in Athevia. Confirm Location Services are enabled and Athevia has location permission.

Does signing out delete my training?

No. Signing out is intentionally separate from deleting local training or cloud backups.

How do I request account-data deletion?

Use the developer support contact listed on Athevia's App Store product page. Until the in-app account deletion control is available, deletion requests are handled through support.
