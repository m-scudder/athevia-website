THE LOOP

Plan less. Train. Review. Adapt.

Athevia’s training engine is designed around a simple loop: prescribe useful work, measure what happened, and shape the next week around reality.

01

Plan

Start with your sport mix, training experience, goals and availability.

02

Train

Follow structured run, ride and swim sessions with clear workout guidance.

03

Measure

Use completed workouts, Health data, GPS, effort and feedback as evidence.

04

Adapt

The next training week is reshaped from what you actually accomplished.
